Char Dham Yatra Guide

An informational guide to the four sacred shrines of Uttarakhand: Yamunotri, Gangotri, Kedarnath, and Badrinath.

About the Pilgrimage

The Char Dham Yatra is traditional pilgrimage circuit located in the high Himalayas of Uttarakhand. Pilgrims traditionally visit the four shrines in a clockwise direction starting from West to East: Yamunotri → Gangotri → Kedarnath → Badrinath. Staying in local village homestays along the route offers pilgrims affordable, authentic rest points away from overcrowded commercial centers.

1. Yamunotri Dham

Yamunotri Temple

Yamunotri is the source of the sacred Yamuna River and the first stop in the traditional Char Dham circuit. Situated at an altitude of 3,291 meters in the Uttarkashi district, the temple is dedicated to Goddess Yamuna. A key highlight here is the Surya Kund, a natural hot water thermal spring near the temple where devotees cook rice and potatoes wrapped in cloth as sacred prasad.

Altitude: 3,291 m Trek: 6 km from Janki Chatti District: Uttarkashi Best Time: May to October
2. Gangotri Dham

Gangotri Temple

Gangotri is the seat of Goddess Ganga and the origin site of the river Bhagirathi (the primary source stream of the Ganges). Nestled among pine forests and towering peaks at 3,100 meters altitude, the temple is built of white granite. Unlike Yamunotri, Gangotri is directly accessible by road, passing through the beautiful pine and apple orchard villages of the Harsil Valley.

Altitude: 3,100 m Access: Direct Road Access Base Town: Harsil / Uttarkashi Best Time: May to October
3. Kedarnath Dham

Kedarnath Temple

Kedarnath is one of the most revered shrines dedicated to Lord Shiva and forms one of the 12 sacred Jyotirlingas in India. Located near the Mandakini River at an altitude of 3,583 meters, it stands against the dramatic backdrop of snow-clad Himalayan peaks. Reaching Kedarnath requires a steep 16-kilometer mountain trek starting from the base camp at Gaurikund.

Altitude: 3,583 m Trek: 16 km from Gaurikund District: Rudraprayag Best Time: May to October
4. Badrinath Dham

Badrinath Temple

Dedicated to Lord Vishnu, Badrinath is the final shrine in the Char Dham circuit. Located on the banks of the Alaknanda River between the Nar and Narayan mountain ranges, the temple features a colorful 50-foot-tall painted facade. It is situated just 3 kilometers from Mana Village—India's first border village—making it a prime hub for both spiritual pilgrimage and rural homestay tourism.

Altitude: 3,133 m Access: Direct Road Access District: Chamoli Best Time: May to October
